I am a Ph.D. graduate in Electrical and Computer Engineering from UC Davis, where I worked under the supervision of Prof. Soheil Ghiasi. I received my B.S. in Electrical Engineering from the University of Tabriz in 2018, joined the Laboratory for Embedded and Programmable Systems at UC Davis later that year as a Graduate Student Researcher, and earned my M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from UC Davis in 2021.

## Research

My work centers on embedded software systems for health applications. A major focus has been the Transabdominal Fetal Pulse Oximetry (TFO) project, where I worked on system design, signal processing, and machine learning for estimating fetal blood oxygen saturation from non-invasive measurements.

## Leadership

I co-led the Storx Technologies team in the 2022 UC Davis Big Bang! Business Competition alongside Regina Hoang. Storx Technologies developed a non-invasive medical device designed to estimate fetal oxygen levels in the womb and help clinicians better assess fetal well-being. Our team won first place and received the top innovation award.

## Other Projects

1. Built ANN models using SafeGraph social-distancing data to predict U.S. COVID-19 incident rates and identify the states with the largest changes in cases.
2. Explored machine learning models on MIMIC-III data to predict ICU length of stay and improve early resource allocation decisions.
3. Implemented Kalman filter estimators for neural decoding of a rodent on a rotating platform using ground reaction force data.
4. Developed a CUDA-based Batcher-Banyan network for bitonic sort and improved throughput over radix sort for smaller random input sizes.
5. Improved IMDb sentiment analysis with transfer learning on BERT using Yelp reviews as auxiliary training data.
6. Applied temporal-difference learning to train a simulated car-control policy for autonomous track navigation.
7. Helped build a panoptic image segmentation pipeline combining DeepLab V2 and Mask R-CNN; the project won a UC Davis poster competition.
8. Built a speech accent classification and transformation pipeline using MFCC features, dynamic time warping, and LPC-based synthesis.
9. Developed an embedded system that differentiated the heart rates of two people using a single sensor on an ARM microcontroller.
10. Implemented monocular ball tracking and catching on a robotic arm with Kalman filtering on Raspberry Pi.
11. Built a Raspberry Pi robot that followed a path in real time using a USB webcam.
12. Implemented a configurable UART module on an Altera FPGA.
